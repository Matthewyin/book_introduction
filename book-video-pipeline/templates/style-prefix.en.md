A collage / scrapbook illustration, mixed-media style. Vertical 9:16 portrait composition.

Style: handmade collage with layered torn paper, soft watercolor washes, gentle pencil linework, washi tape accents at the edges, subtle kraft and photo-paper textures. Figures are paper-cut silhouettes with soft shadow depth. Warm, nostalgic, intimate — like a page from a personal journal. Muted, harmonious, cohesive palette throughout.

Color palette (use only these five): cream paper #F5F1EA, soft pink #E8C4C4, muted blue #A8C0D0, warm gold paper #E8C37A, kraft brown #C4A882. Low saturation. Do not introduce colors outside this palette.

Character rendering: paper-cut collage figures with simple hair and clothing shapes. Faces are minimal — a few soft lines at most, no detailed features. Emotion is carried by posture, shoulder line, and body language, not by facial expression. Keep faces soft, young, and stylized; never photorealistic, never aged, never harsh.

Composition: main subject in the upper two-thirds. The bottom third must stay visually quiet — no subject, no high-contrast detail — reserved for Chinese subtitle overlay.

Negative: no readable text, no watermark, no UI elements, no logos, no realistic photography, no 3D render, no oil painting or thick impasto brushwork, no flat vector art, no neon, no harsh clashing colors, no busy noisy patterns.
