# Style card: watercolor-cat
# 参考图：附件图4（水彩质感猫吃西瓜）
# 备用风格：当前主力为人物线（cute-anime-girl）。本卡作为萌宠线预留，需验证后再启用。

A watercolor picture-book illustration featuring a cute anthropomorphic cat. Vertical 9:16 portrait composition.

Style: soft watercolor washes on textured paper, gentle wet-on-wet gradients, delicate pencil linework underneath, warm dappled light, tiny hand-painted speckles and soft blooms. The overall feeling is cozy, storybook-like, nostalgic and tender — like an illustration from a children's book or a pet-themed stationery collection.

Color palette (use only these five, keep low saturation): cream paper #F5F1EA, soft pink #E8C4C4, muted blue #A8C0D0, warm gold #E8C37A, kraft brown #C4A882. Warm gold and cream dominate the light; muted blue and kraft brown carry shadows and quiet moments; pink is reserved for tiny accents like blush, nose, or props.

Character rendering: a small fluffy tabby or cream-colored cat, upright on two hind legs, anthropomorphic but still clearly a cat. Round head, large gentle eyes, small pink nose, soft whiskers, slightly oversized paws. Body language conveys emotion: slumped shoulders for tiredness, perked ears for curiosity, curled tail for comfort. Fur is soft watercolor texture, not hyper-realistic fur rendering.

Composition: full-frame vertical composition. The subject may extend through the entire 9:16 frame. No mandatory bottom whitespace; subtitles are handled by a gradient scrim layer at composition time.

Negative: no realistic photography, no 3D render, no thick oil painting, no flat vector art, no hyper-realistic individual fur strands, no neon, no harsh clashing colors, no readable text, no watermark, no UI elements, no busy noisy patterns.
