# 铁律：暖调、治愈、安静。禁止冷色蓝绿、禁止清爽阳光感。

Warm-toned still-life photography style, cozy and intimate, vertical 9:16 portrait composition.

Style: soft warm-toned photography, gentle natural lighting, shallow depth of field, subtle warm film grain. The mood is warm, healing, cozy, and bedtime-quiet — like a late-night reading nook or a peaceful evening moment. NOT bright, NOT sunny, NOT cool-toned.

## Color palette (warm-toned, MUST use these, low saturation)
Warm cream #F5EBD8, warm amber #D4A574, warm brown #8B6F47, soft rose #D4B8B0, warm white #FFF8F0.
Warm cream and warm amber carry the emotional temperature; warm brown for shadows and depth; soft rose as a tiny accent. The overall tone is distinctly WARM — like candlelight, sunset, or an amber reading lamp.

**MUST be warm-toned. NO cool colors, NO blue, NO green, NO mint, NO teal, NO cyan. The palette must read as warm and cozy.**

## Composition: minimalist warm still-life
A simple, intimate scene with 2-4 objects arranged on a warm wooden surface:
- An open book (warm pages, soft shadow)
- A warm amber reading lamp or candle (soft glow, gentle light pool)
- A ceramic mug of tea or coffee (gentle steam)
- Optional: a small green plant (just a touch, leaves catching warm light), dried flowers, or autumn leaves

No people, no characters, no hands, no text in the image. The scene is quiet and empty of human presence — the warmth comes from the objects and light.

## Lighting
- Main light: a single warm source (lamp / candle / sunset through window) casting a soft golden glow
- Shadows: warm brown tones, soft edges, never harsh
- Bokeh: creamy warm light spots in the background
- Overall: amber/golden warmth, like the golden hour or candlelight

## Mood keywords
warm, cozy, healing, bedtime, quiet, intimate, nostalgic, peaceful, tender, amber, candlelight

## Negative
no people, no characters, no hands, no anime, no illustration, no 3D render, no flat vector, no neon, no cool colors, no blue, no green, no mint, no teal, no cyan, no bright sunny daylight, no harsh shadows, no high contrast, no readable text, no watermark, no UI elements.
